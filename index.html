FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to pay before I see anything?

Payment is taken when you sign up. For the $249 website, $62.25 is a non-refundable deposit for work done; the rest is refundable until go-live. For the Get Found pilot, you pay $298.75 at signup, including a $99.75 non-refundable deposit. (Pending lawyer review.) We're not taking payments yet.

What does $249 include?

Your finished one-page site, hosting for 12 months, a free readysite subdomain or connection to a domain you own, a click-to-call button, and up to 2 rounds of text edits before go-live.

What happens after year one?

Hosting renews at $99 per year. You can cancel renewal anytime, and the site goes offline at the end of the paid year. If you forget to cancel, tell us within 14 days of a renewal charge and we refund it.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, partly. Before your site goes live, we refund everything except the $62.25 non-refundable deposit (25% of $249) for work already done. See the Refund Policy.

Where does the information come from?

From the details you send us: name, phone, hours, services and areas you serve. We can change or remove anything you like.

Do you review replies with me first?

Yes. On Get Found, review replies and Google profile posts go live only after you approve them.

Do I need my own domain?

No. You can use a free subdomain. If you own a domain, we send simple written steps to connect it.
