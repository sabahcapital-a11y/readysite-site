FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to pay before I see anything?

No. Your site is already built. You look at the live preview first and only pay if you want to keep it.

What does $249 include?

Your finished one-page site, hosting for 12 months, a free readysite subdomain or connection to a domain you own, a click-to-call button, and up to 2 rounds of text edits before go-live.

What happens after year one?

Hosting renews at $99 per year, billed automatically by Stripe. You can cancel anytime from the link in your receipt, and the site goes offline at the end of the paid year.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you're not satisfied before your site goes live, we refund you in full. Just email us.

Where does the information come from?

From your public business listing: name, phone, hours, services and short quotes from public reviews. We can change or remove anything you like.

Do I need my own domain?

No. You can use a free subdomain. If you own a domain, we send simple written steps to connect it.

Is the AI a real person?

No. It always says it's an automated AI assistant, and callers can ask for a callback from your team. Calls are recorded only with a disclosure at the start.

Will the AI call or text people who never contacted me?

No. It only answers inbound calls and texts back people who just called you. Every text includes opt-out instructions.

What's the difference between the three AI plans?

Missed-Call Rescue texts back callers you miss. AI Front Desk adds an AI voice receptionist for inbound calls. Front Desk Autopilot adds quote and lead follow-up, Google profile posts, a job-software hookup and a monthly report.

Do you send marketing texts to my old customer list?

Not without proof of written consent. The win-back program is email first, and texts go only to customers who opted in in writing.

Will invoice reminders sound like debt collection?

No. They're friendly reminders from your business about your own invoices, with a pay link. No threats or collection-agency language.

I don't want this preview. What do I do?

Reply "remove" or use the opt-out link in our email, and we take the preview down and won't contact you again.
