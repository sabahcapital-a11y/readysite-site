Asking for Google reviews: a guide for OKC roofers

The simplest way for an Oklahoma City roofer to ask for reviews is to send every customer the same short request with your Google review link after the job is done. Don't screen out unhappy customers, don't offer anything in return, and reply to reviews.

Sample request (text to a customer who hired you)

Hi {First name}, thanks for choosing {Your Roofing Co} for your roof. If you have a minute, we'd appreciate an honest review: {review link}. Thank you!

Send only to customers you worked with, and only by a channel they agreed to.

Timing

  • Send it after final cleanup and inspection, not mid-job.
  • One reminder at most.

Rules (FTC 2024 Reviews Rule and Google policy)

  • No review gating, so everyone gets the same link.
  • No incentives of any kind.
  • No reviews from owners, staff or family (Google treats these as a conflict of interest; the FTC rule also covers undisclosed insider reviews).
  • Never write or buy reviews.
