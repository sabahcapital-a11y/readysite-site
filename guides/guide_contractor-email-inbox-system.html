How contractors can get their email inbox under control (without hiring an assistant)

Quote requests, schedule changes, warranty questions, invoices and a pile of vendor pitches all land in the same inbox. Here is a simple system any service business can run, and how ReadySite can set it up for you.

1. Use six labels, not sixty

Emergency, Needs You, Quote, Schedule, Billing, Warranty. Everything a customer sends fits one of them. Vendor offers and SEO pitches get their own label so they stop burying real jobs.

2. Draft, don't auto-send

AI is good at a first draft built from your price list and policies. It still makes mistakes, so the safe setup keeps every reply in Drafts until a person sends it.

3. Only three things should interrupt you

Emergencies, refund requests and upset customers. Everything else can wait for a short list each weekday morning.

4. Every promise becomes a task

"I'll get you that quote by Friday" is where jobs get lost. Write it down as a task with one name and one due date. A free task board or a shared spreadsheet is enough.

5. Leave the backlog alone

Start sorting from today forward. Trying to clean years of old mail first is why most inbox projects stall.
