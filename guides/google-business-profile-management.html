How it works

1. Join the waitlist. 2. You add us as a manager of your Google Business Profile using Google's own sharing tools; we never ask for your password. 3. In the first 2 weeks we fix categories, attributes, services and the description and send you the 90-day plan. 4. Each week we draft a post and any review replies for you to approve.

What you do

Give manager access to your profile, approve posts and replies, and take the photos on the photo plan. Changes Google must verify, such as a new address or name, may need you to complete Google's own verification.

Results check by day 60

By day 60 we show you your real numbers from your own Google profile and our logs, such as reviews gained and calls or inquiries logged. Real numbers only, never estimates, and no guarantee of rankings, calls or jobs. Google decides how profiles rank.

Good to know

We follow Google's guidelines: no fake reviews, no review gating, no keyword-stuffed business names. ReadySite is not affiliated with Google. Results vary by business.
