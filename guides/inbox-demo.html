Safety rules

  • Drafts only. Replies are saved as drafts. It never sends an email; you review and send every one.
  • No passwords. We never ask for your email password. You grant access yourself inside your own Google account and can remove it anytime.
  • Emails can't give it orders. Instructions written inside an incoming email are treated as text to sort, not commands. An email can't make it send, delete, pay or forward anything.
  • No copies kept. We don't keep copies of your customers' mail; it stays in your account.

AI can sort or draft incorrectly, so you review everything before it goes out. Email only. Results vary by business.
