AI receptionist for salons

An AI receptionist for salon businesses answers inbound calls automatically, tells callers it's an automated assistant, collects the job details, and passes them to you. When you miss a call, it texts the caller back with your booking link so callers aren't left hanging. It only talks to people who contacted you first.

What it does

  • Answers inbound calls automatically as an AI assistant and passes the details to you.
  • Tells callers it's an automated AI assistant at the start of every call, and that the call may be recorded.
  • Texts callers back automatically when you miss a call, with your booking link (Missed-Call Rescue).
  • Asks the basics ("Cut, color, or styling?"), collects name, number, and address, and flags urgent jobs.
  • Works only with people who call or text you first. No cold calls or cold texts.
  • You can review every conversation and take over anytime.

Example: booking a Saturday appointment

Caller rings, nobody can pick up.
Text (seconds later): Hi, this is the automated assistant for {Your Salon Co}. Sorry we missed your call! What can we help with? You can also book here: {link}. Reply STOP to opt out.
Caller: Booking a saturday appointment.
Assistant: Thanks. I've sent your details to the team, and someone will call you back.

Sample conversation for illustration with a fictional business.

What it won't do

  • Quote binding prices, diagnose problems, or give safety advice. Emergencies are directed to 911 or your emergency line.
  • Pretend to be a person.
  • Call or text anyone who didn't contact you first.

FAQ

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It discloses that it's an automated AI assistant at the start of every call and in the first text.

Does it make outbound calls?

No. It only answers inbound calls and texts back numbers that just called you.

Can I take over a conversation?

Yes. You can review every conversation and take over anytime.
