Review request templates for text and email

The best review request is short: the customer's name, a thank-you for the specific job, one clear ask for an honest review, your direct Google review link, and (for texts) an opt-out line. Send it to every customer within a few hours of the job, and send at most one reminder.

Copy-paste templates

1. Same-day text

Hi {first name}, thanks for choosing {business} today! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps a small business a lot: {link}
Reply STOP to opt out.

2. Personal text (mention the job)

Hi {first name}, it was great getting your {job} done today. If you have a minute, we'd really appreciate an honest Google review: {link}
Thanks, {your name} at {business}
Reply STOP to opt out.

3. Email

Subject: Thanks from {business}, quick favor?

Hi {first name},
Thank you for trusting us with {job}. Reviews help neighbors find a local business they can trust. Would you share your honest experience on Google? It takes about a minute:
{link}

Thank you,
{business}

4. One reminder (send once, ~3 days later)

Hi {first name}, just a quick reminder in case this got buried. If you'd like to share your experience with {business}, here's the link: {link}
No worries either way, and thanks again!
Reply STOP to opt out.

5. In person / counter script

"Thanks for coming in today. We're a small local business, so Google reviews really help us. If you have a minute, this card has a QR code that takes you straight there. We'd love your honest feedback."

6. Reply to a positive review

Thank you, {first name}! We're glad the {job} went smoothly. We appreciate you choosing a local business.

7. Reply to a negative review

Hi {first name}, we're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. We'd like to make it right. Please call us at {phone} or email {email} and ask for {name}.

Email subject lines

  • Thanks from {business}, quick favor?
  • How did we do, {first name}?
  • Would you share your experience?
  • A minute to help a local business?

Rules that keep these templates safe

  • Ask every customer. Google prohibits selectively soliciting positive reviews ("review gating"). Don't send happy customers to Google and unhappy ones to a private form.
  • No incentives. No discounts, gifts, or contest entries in exchange for reviews.
  • Ask for an honest review, never "5 stars".
  • Texts: only to customers who gave their number and agreed to messages; include "Reply STOP to opt out" and honor it right away.
  • In the U.S., the FTC's 2024 rule on consumer reviews (16 CFR Part 465) bans fake reviews and review suppression.

Sources: Google prohibited & restricted content; FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule. This page is general information, not legal advice.

FAQ

Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's policies don't allow incentives for reviews, including discounts, gifts, or contest entries.

Can I only ask happy customers?

No. Asking only customers you expect to be positive is review gating, which Google prohibits. Ask every customer the same way.

How many reminders should I send?

One polite reminder a few days later is reasonable. After that, stop.
