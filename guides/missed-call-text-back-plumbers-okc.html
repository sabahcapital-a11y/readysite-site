Missed-call text-back (not available in Oklahoma) for plumbers in Oklahoma City

For an Oklahoma City plumber, missed-call text-back (not available in Oklahoma) means anyone whose call you couldn't answer gets a text within seconds. The text names itself as your automated assistant, asks what's going on, and shares your booking link. Urgent jobs like a burst pipe get flagged to you right away.

Missed-call texting isn't offered in Oklahoma; we set up email and website follow-up instead.

How it works

  1. A customer calls and you can't pick up.
  2. They get a text from your business number saying it's your automated assistant.
  3. Their reply ("water heater leaking") is sent to you as an alert.
  4. You call back, or take over the text thread yourself.

Example text

Hi, this is the automated assistant for {Your Plumbing Co}. Sorry we missed your call! Is this a leak, a clog, or a water heater? You can also book here: {link}. Reply STOP to opt out.
Caller: Pipe burst under the sink.
Assistant: Thanks, I've flagged this as urgent and sent it to the team. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

Sample conversation with a fictional business. The assistant doesn't diagnose or quote prices.

What it won't do

  • Text anyone who didn't contact you first.
  • Pretend to be a person.
  • Quote binding prices or give safety advice beyond pointing to 911.
