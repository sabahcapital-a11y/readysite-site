For an Oklahoma City plumber, missed-call follow-up means a caller you couldn't answer still hears back fast. Your voicemail greeting and website point them to a short form; when they fill it out or leave an email, they get an automated email reply that says it's your automated assistant, asks what's going on, and shares your booking link. Urgent jobs like a burst pipe get flagged to you right away.

Missed-call texting is not offered in Oklahoma; we set up email and website follow-up instead.

How it works

  1. A customer calls and you can't pick up.
  2. Your voicemail greeting points them to your website form (or they leave an email address).
  3. They get an email from your business saying it's your automated assistant, with your booking link.
  4. Their details ("water heater leaking") are sent to you as an alert, and you call back or reply yourself.

Example email

Subject: We missed your call, {Your Plumbing Co}
Hi, this is the automated assistant for {Your Plumbing Co}. Sorry we missed your call! Is this a leak, a clog, or a water heater? Reply to this email or book here: {link}.
Customer: Pipe burst under the sink.
Assistant: Thanks, I've flagged this as urgent and sent it to the team. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

Sample conversation with a fictional business.

Rules it follows

  • Replies only to people who contacted you first. No cold emails to your customers.
  • Every email says it's an automated assistant and never pretends to be a person.
  • No binding price quotes or diagnoses.
  • You can review every conversation and take over anytime.
