Handling storm-season calls for roofers in Tulsa and OKC

After a hail or wind storm in Tulsa or Oklahoma City, roofing phones get busy all at once. An AI assistant can answer inbound calls automatically and text back missed callers. It says it's automated, collects the address and the kind of damage, and passes the details to you so callers aren't left hanging.

Missed-call texting isn't offered in Oklahoma; we set up email and website follow-up instead.

What to collect on every storm call

  • Name, callback number and property address.
  • Type of damage: active leak, missing shingles, hail dents, or an inspection request.
  • Whether there's water coming in now (flagged urgent).
  • Whether they've filed an insurance claim. The assistant just records this and doesn't give insurance advice.

Example text

Hi, this is the automated assistant for {Your Roofing Co}. Sorry we missed your call! Is this a leak, storm damage, or an estimate? You can also book an inspection here: {link}. Reply STOP to opt out.

Sample with a fictional business.

Stay on the right side of the rules

  • Inbound only. Don't text or call storm-area lists that never contacted you.
  • Calls are recorded only with a notice at the start.
  • No promises about insurance outcomes or prices by text.
