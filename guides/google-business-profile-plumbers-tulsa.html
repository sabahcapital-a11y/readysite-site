Google Business Profile tips for plumbers in Tulsa

For a Tulsa plumber, a Google Business Profile works best when it's accurate and kept up to date. Use the right primary category, real hours, the service area you actually cover, your own photos, regular posts, and replies to every review. Ask every customer for a review the same way, with no screening and no incentives.

Missed-call texting isn't offered in Oklahoma; we set up email and website follow-up instead.

Profile basics

  • Primary category: Plumber. Google says to use as few categories as possible and pick ones that complete "this business IS a".
  • Service area: the towns you really serve. If you work from home, hide your home address; Google says a service area generally shouldn't extend past about 2 hours' drive from your base.
  • Hours, including how you handle after-hours calls.
  • Photos of your own vans, crew and jobs, with customer permission.
  • Same business name, phone and website as your site, with no keywords or taglines added to the name (Google can suspend profiles that do this).

Reviews the right way

  • Send a review request to every customer, happy or not. Google doesn't allow businesses to selectively ask for positive reviews.
  • No discounts, gift cards or entries for reviews.
  • Reply to reviews, especially where you can add something helpful, and never share a reviewer's private information.
