Answering service vs AI receptionist: what it really costs

For a small local business, a live answering service with human receptionists, such as Smith.ai, publicly starts at $300/month for 30 calls (checked October 2026). AI options price differently, so compare total cost, and any AI should disclose that it's AI and be reviewed by the owner. A person is best for complex calls; AI fits after-hours and overflow. How to choose

  1. Count your missed calls for two weeks (your phone's call log shows them).
  2. Decide what callers need: a booking and callback (AI works well) or detailed human judgment (use people).
  3. Check disclosure and consent: any AI should say it's AI; recorded calls need a recording notice.
  4. Avoid long contracts until you've tested it for a month.

Honest limits of AI receptionists

  • AI can make mistakes; you should review conversations.
  • It shouldn't quote binding prices or give safety, legal, or medical advice.
  • Some callers will prefer a person; offer a callback.

FAQ

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than an answering service?

Often per month, but compare total cost including setup, minutes, and how many calls need a human.

Does an AI receptionist replace staff?

We don't claim that. It answers inbound calls and passes details to you; many businesses use it for after-hours and overflow.
