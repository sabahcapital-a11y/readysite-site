Why your Google Business Profile isn't showing up (fix list for Oklahoma contractors)

The most common reasons a contractor's Business Profile doesn't show: it isn't verified yet (Google says verification reviews can take up to 5 business days), it was suspended for breaking Google's guidelines, the business name has extra keywords, the category or service area is off, or it simply ranks below closer or better-known businesses for that search.

Check these in order

  1. Is it verified? Open your profile while signed in. If it asks you to get verified, finish that first. Google says that after you complete verification, its review can take up to 5 business days, occasionally longer. If you verify by postcard, don't edit your name, address or category while you wait, and don't request a new code: either one can make the code in the mail stop working (Google: verify your business).
  2. Is it suspended or disabled? Google may suspend profiles that don't follow its guidelines. The fix is an appeal through Google's appeals tool, not a new profile. Google says not to create a new profile for the same business (Google: fix suspended or disabled profiles).
  3. Is the name your real-world name? Google says the name should match what's on your trucks, website and paperwork. Adding extra words such as "Best AC Repair Tulsa" isn't allowed and can get the profile suspended (Google: guidelines for representing your business).
  4. Service area vs address. Most HVAC, plumbing and roofing companies go to the customer. Google says a service-area business should hide its address, for example a plumber working from home, and keep one profile with a service area. You can add up to 20 service areas, Google says the overall area generally shouldn't go beyond about 2 hours' drive from your base, and edits can take up to 48 hours to show (Google: manage service areas; guidelines).
  5. Category and details. Google ranks on relevance, distance and prominence. To help with relevance, Google says to give complete and detailed business info: the right primary category, hours, phone, services and website (Google: improve your local ranking).
  6. Reviews missing? Google checks new reviews against its policies, which can delay them a few days. It removes reviews that break its policies and won't restore them. Reviews can also go missing after a profile is reinstated, and Google says to contact support if that happens (Google: missing or delayed reviews).
  7. Showing, just not for you? Distance matters. A search from across town can show different businesses than one near your shop. You can't pay Google to rank higher in local results (Google).

Quick checklist

  • Verified, with no "get verified" prompt
  • Real business name only
  • Address hidden if customers don't come to you
  • Service areas set to towns you actually serve (see our local pages)
  • Primary category matches your main trade
  • Hours, phone and website filled in and correct

FAQ

Why isn't my profile showing after verification?

Google says verification reviews can take up to 5 business days, and occasionally longer. If it still doesn't show after that, check for a suspension notice and confirm your name, category and service area follow Google's guidelines.

Should a service-area contractor show an address?

Google says service-area businesses should hide their address and set a service area instead. A plumber working from home, for example, should clear the address.

Can I pay Google to show up higher on Maps?

No. Google says you can't pay for better local ranking. It ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence.
