Missed-call text-back for HVAC companies in Tulsa

Missed-call text-back sends an automatic text to anyone who called your HVAC company and didn't reach you. The text says it's an automated assistant for your business, asks what they need, shares your booking link, and includes "Reply STOP to opt out." It only goes to numbers that just called you.

When it helps a Tulsa HVAC shop

  • You're on a roof or in an attic and can't answer.
  • Calls come in after hours during a heat wave or a cold snap.
  • Two calls come in at once and one goes to voicemail.

In each case, callers get a reply so they aren't left hanging, and you get an alert with the details.

Example text

Hi, this is the automated assistant for {Your HVAC Co}. Sorry we missed your call! What can we help with? You can also book here: {link}. Reply STOP to opt out.
Caller: AC stopped blowing cold.
Assistant: Thanks. I've sent your details to the team, and someone will call you back. Is this cooling, heating, or maintenance? If anyone is in danger or you smell gas, call 911.

Sample conversation with a fictional business.

Rules it follows

  • Works only with people who call or text you first. No cold texts.
  • STOP is honored immediately. Industry guidelines call for standard "STOP" opt-out wording (CTIA), and the FCC says opt-out requests must be honored promptly (FCC); we do it right away.
  • No binding price quotes or diagnoses.
  • You can review every conversation and take over anytime.
  • Texting needs carrier (A2P 10DLC) registration before go-live (The Campaign Registry), and that's part of setup.
