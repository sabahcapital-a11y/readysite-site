How to find your Google review link (and make a QR code)

Sign in to the Google account that manages your Business Profile, search your business name on Google (or open your profile in Google Maps), select Read reviews, then Get more reviews (some profiles show "Ask for reviews"), and select Copy. On a computer, the same window also offers a QR code you can save. Test both before use.

On a computer

  1. Sign in to the Google account that owns or manages your Business Profile.
  2. Search your exact business name on Google. Your profile tools appear at the top.
  3. Select Read reviews, then Get more reviews (some profiles show Ask for reviews; labels change over time).
  4. Select Copy. Save the link in a note.
  5. To save Google's QR code, right-click it and choose Save image as.

On a phone

  1. Open the Google Maps app, signed in as the owner.
  2. Tap your profile picture and open your Business Profile.
  3. Look for Get more reviews or Ask for reviews, then Share or Copy. Labels move between app versions; if you can't find it, use a computer.

Google says its review QR codes can only be generated in a computer browser, not on mobile devices.

Make a QR code

  • Easiest: use the QR code in Google's Get more reviews window (computer only).
  • Otherwise paste the link into a reputable QR generator and download a high-resolution PNG.
  • Prefer a static QR code that contains your link directly. Some "dynamic" free codes redirect through a third party and can stop working later.

Where to put it

  • Thank-you text or email after every job (templates)
  • Email signature and invoice footer
  • Counter card, receipts, and the back of business cards

Share it with every customer, and never offer anything in exchange for a review.

FAQ

Why can't I see the Get more reviews button?

You must be signed in with an account that owns or manages a verified Business Profile. Labels also change; look for any reviews option in your profile tools.

Does the review link expire?

Google doesn't publish an expiry for it. Test it every so often, especially after profile changes.
