# ReadySite Independent Pricing Optimization — 2026-10-20
Status: Research recommendation; NOT approved, NOT deployed, NOT payment-ready.
Scope: ReadySite only; no other Grokbot venture decisions.

## Recommendation
**Website Launch: $599 setup + $39/month care**, with the monthly plan separately and accurately described. This is a testable hypothesis, not validated willingness to pay.

Includes one mobile-friendly, accessible website page; up to five service summaries; contact and click-to-call links; business-supplied and rights-cleared photos; one pre-launch revision round; a basic technical check; hosting and uptime checks subject to actual implementation; and up to one small text update per month. Excludes custom domain registration, email accounts, guaranteed rankings/leads, e-commerce, paid ads, AI reception, CRM, compliance-sensitive automation, and extensive design changes.

No mandatory long contract proposed; exact cancellation, export/ownership, billing start, content rights, refunds, accessibility and service levels require explicit final agreement and legal review. Site is not sold until a contact channel, payment path, contract and production delivery have been tested.

## Why replace $249 + $99/year
- $249 initial price risks absorbing discovery, revision, QA, customer service and support time with weak gross dollars.
- $99/year renewal is only $8.25/month before payment processing, domain coordination, customer requests and uptime work.
- $599 setup supports verified actual production work; $39/month separates continuing service and recurring income.
- Avoid deceptive comparative savings or claims of high ROI without observed customer outcomes.

## Model — illustrative not actual
Assume setup labor 4 hours at $50/hour, plus $15 direct setup expense, plus payment processing 2.9% + 30 cents:
$599 setup less $200 labor less $15 expense less ~$17.67 payment fees = ~$366.33 initial contribution BEFORE overhead/taxes/customer acquisition.
Monthly: $39 less ~$1.43 payment fees less $5 hosting/tools allocation less 0.3 hour support at $50/hour = ~$17.57 contribution per month BEFORE overhead, CAC and taxes.
Year-one gross billings: $599 + 12*$39 = $1,067. Estimated first-year contribution under assumptions = ~$577.17. Actual hours, provider costs, refunds and retention could make this worse or negative.
This assumes 12 full monthly billings and is NOT an ARR or income guarantee. If month-to-month clients cancel early, year-one billings drop.

## Price experiment
Test in owner-approved compliant outreach or inbound inquiries with 15–30 genuinely qualified prospects; do not treat these as statistical power.
A: $599 setup + $39/month, proposed primary.
B: $799 setup + $29/month, higher initial cash and fewer retained-care dollars.
C: $399 setup + $59/month, easier initial entry and higher ongoing commitment.
Use equivalent *scope* and clear first-year total: A $1,067, B $1,147, C $1,107, to avoid confusing setup and recurring components.
Measure qualified interest, price objection, proposal acceptance, initial payment, implementation time, support use, cancellation and refunds.
No false discount, manufactured urgency, pay-to-review schemes, outcome guarantees, or hidden renewal costs.
After three delivered paying pilots, revise using realized unit economics; avoid declaring PMF.
