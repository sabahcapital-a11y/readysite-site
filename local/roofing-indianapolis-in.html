FAQ

Can it handle a lot of calls after a storm?

Missed-call text-back replies to each missed caller automatically, so no one waits on hold for a callback. You can review every conversation and take over anytime.

We don't sell leads. The optional permit lead alerts add-on ($149/month) lists new building permits where the city publishes permit data, for mail and research only, not cold calls or texts. Indianapolis is in Marion County.

No. It says it's an automated AI assistant in the first text and at the start of every call, and callers can ask for a callback from your team.

Will it text or call people who never contacted me?

No. It only answers inbound calls and texts back people who just called you. Every first text includes "Reply STOP to opt out."
